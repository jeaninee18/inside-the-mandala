Chapter one
The Invitation

An hour that belongs to you

Most of us move through the day slightly ahead of ourselves, already braced for what is next. It is rare to give the body a full hour with nothing asked of it.

That is what this hour is. The Zeidler Dome goes dark, live sound fills the room, and mandalas turn slowly across the whole ceiling above you. There is nothing to follow and nowhere to be. Most people feel their breath drop within the first few minutes.

Come as you are. Nothing is required of you here.

Chapter two
What to Expect

A full-sensory immersion

You will be reclined for the full hour while sound and light move around you under the 360° dome. There is nothing to follow and nothing to do.

Mandala Sky

Mandalas and sacred geometry turn slowly across the full dome overhead. Watching them gives the mind something gentle to rest on.

Live Sound

Crystal bowls, gongs, and voice, played live in the room and carried through the dome's surround system. Low tones you feel in your chest as much as hear.

Nervous System Reset

The hour is paced to move you out of a stress response and into the deep rest where the body does its repair work.

Reclined & Held

The dome seats recline, so you can settle back and stay there the whole hour. No posture to hold, no instructions to follow.

Chapter three
Your Guides

Jeanine & Matt

This is the first time The Mindful Mandala Wellness has brought a sound bath into the Zeidler Dome at TELUS World of Science - Edmonton. Jeanine and Matt have built the hour together with one aim, that the room feels safe enough for you to fully let go.

Jeanine

Sound Alchemist · Founder of The Mindful Mandala Wellness

Jeanine's work with sound began in Mexico, where she first felt how much vibration could settle the body. She has been guiding sound baths since 2021, across hundreds of sessions, and now brings that practice home to Edmonton through The Mindful Mandala Wellness. She stays attentive to the room from the first note to the last, adjusting as she goes, so you can stop managing yourself for an hour.

Matt

Live Sound · Audio Engineer

Matt is a lifelong musician and a professional audio engineer who has spent years working live rooms. He mixes the dome so the sound stays full and warm without ever getting loud enough to jar you, and he watches the levels throughout the hour so nothing catches you off guard.
