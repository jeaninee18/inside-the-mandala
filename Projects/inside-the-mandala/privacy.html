Privacy

Your privacy

Last updated November 11, 2026

Inside the Mandala is a live sound bath under the Zeidler Dome at TELUS World of Science - Edmonton, presented by The Mindful Mandala Wellness in Edmonton, Alberta. This page explains, in plain language, what information this website collects, why we collect it, who helps us handle it and the choices you have.

What we collect

  • Your email address, when you join our email list through a signup form on this site.
  • Your name, email and order details, which our ticketing provider shares with us when you book a ticket.
  • How the site is used, such as the pages you view, the type of device and browser you use, your approximate location (city or region) and the site or link that brought you here. This is gathered through Google Analytics and is not used to identify you personally.
  • Anything you choose to send us, for example when you email us with a question.

We never ask for payment details on this site, and we only collect what we need to run the event and stay in touch with you.

How we use it

We use your information to send you details about Inside the Mandala and future events from The Mindful Mandala Wellness, to manage your booking and answer your questions, and to understand how people find and use this site so we can make it better. We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information.

Email updates

We only email people who have signed up or booked with us. Every email includes an unsubscribe link, and you can also ask us to remove you at any time by writing to hello@themindfulmandalawellness.com.

Who helps us

We use a few trusted services to run this site and the event, and each handles your information under its own privacy policy:

  • Mailchimp (Intuit) stores our email list and sends our emails.
  • Eventbrite sells tickets and processes payments. Your payment details are entered and stored with them, not with us.
  • Google Analytics measures site visits.
  • Vercel hosts this website and keeps standard technical logs, such as IP addresses, to keep it secure and running.
  • Google Fonts supplies the typefaces on this page, which means your browser connects to Google when the page loads.

Some of these providers store information outside Canada, including in the United States, where it may be subject to the laws of that country.

Cookies and analytics

Google Analytics uses cookies, which are small files saved by your browser, to count visits and understand how the site is used. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, or install Google's Analytics opt-out add-on. The site works the same either way.

How long we keep it

We keep your email address until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete it. Booking details are kept for as long as we need them for the event and for our business and tax records, and analytics data is kept for the period set in our Google Analytics account.

Your choices

You can ask us at any time to show you the personal information we hold about you, to correct it, to delete it or to stop using it for email. Write to hello@themindfulmandalawellness.com and we will take care of it. If you have a concern we have not resolved, you can contact the Office of the Information and Privacy Commissioner of Alberta or the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.

Links to other sites

This site links to Eventbrite, Instagram and other websites. Their privacy practices are their own, so we encourage you to read their policies when you visit them.

Changes to this page

If we change how we handle your information, we will update this page and the date at the top.

Contact

The Mindful Mandala Wellness, Edmonton, Alberta. Questions about your privacy are always welcome at hello@themindfulmandalawellness.com, and we will respond personally.
