Privacy

Your privacy

Inside the Mandala is a sound & light dome journey presented by The Mindful Mandala Wellness in Edmonton, Alberta. This notice explains, in plain language, how we handle the information you share with us through this site.

What we collect

If you join our email list, we collect the email address you provide. If you purchase a ticket, that transaction is handled by our ticketing provider, and we receive your name and order details from them. We do not collect more than we need.

How we use it

We use your email only to share details about this event and future journeys from The Mindful Mandala Wellness. We do not sell, rent, or trade your information. You can unsubscribe at any time using the link in any email we send.

Ticketing & payments

Tickets and payments are processed by our third-party ticketing provider. Your payment details are entered and stored with them, not with us. Please review your ticketing provider's own privacy terms at checkout.

Your choices

You may ask us to show you, correct, or delete the information we hold about you at any time. Just reach out and we will take care of it.

Contact

Questions about your privacy? Email hello@insidethemandala.com and we will respond personally.
